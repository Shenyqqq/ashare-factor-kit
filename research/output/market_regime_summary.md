## Training window notes

- Weekly regime switches ~every **1 weeks** (~0.3 months); a **6-month** train window captures ~20 regime segment(s).
- **12-month** window spans ~40 switches — often mixes bull/chop/bear; good when regimes are short-lived.
- **24-month** window risks stale bear/chop samples when dominated by weak/chop years [].
- **Practical**: h5 weekly rebalance → prefer **6–12m** train; h20 monthly → **12m** default OK, consider **6m** after extended 弱势/震荡.
